Who provides TapMyHours

TapMyHours is provided by Nikolaj Brandt, Denmark (Everwise Projects). For questions about privacy or to exercise your rights, email everwiseprojects@gmail.com. For personal information we receive or control, Nikolaj Brandt is the responsible provider.

Your work records

Work sessions, start and finish times, breaks, duration-only entries, notes, work schedules, opening balances, hourly rates, selected currencies, and reminder preferences are stored locally in the app. Calculations are performed using those records. We do not operate a user-account system or cloud synchronisation service for your work history, and these work records are not sent to RevenueCat for purchase validation.

Your device settings may include app data in Apple-managed device backups. This is separate from a TapMyHours account or developer-operated cloud service.

Apple and RevenueCat

Apple processes in-app payments. RevenueCat helps retrieve products, validate purchases, restore access, and manage Pro entitlements. This involves an app-user identifier, purchase and transaction history, entitlement status, and technical information needed to communicate with the service, such as app/platform information and network requests. An app-user identifier may be generated anonymously; no TapMyHours login is required.

Purchase services may contact their servers before you buy Pro. Purchase information is also used for purchase reporting and service operation. We do not receive your payment-card details, and we do not use purchase information for advertising across other companies’ apps.

Read RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy for their processing.

Reminders and permissions

Daily reminders are optional and scheduled locally with notification permission. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings. Enabling a reminder does not send your work records to a remote push-notification service.

Exports and backups

PDF and CSV reports contain the work information selected for export, which may include notes, work dates, durations, schedules, and estimated earnings. JSON backup files contain saved app data for restoration. Exported files can reveal personal or employment information.

Files are shared or saved to the destination you choose; we do not upload them to our own server. If you choose email or cloud storage, that provider’s privacy terms apply. Keep backups in a safe place. Delete exported copies and cloud backups separately when you no longer need them.

When you contact support

If you email everwiseprojects@gmail.com, we receive your email address, correspondence, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues, and resolve requests. Please remove private work details from screenshots and do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Support information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, follow up on an issue, resolve a dispute, or meet applicable legal obligations. Email is handled through our email provider.

Advertising, analytics, and this website

The current app contains no advertising SDK or separate behavioural analytics service. RevenueCat still processes purchase information as described above. Apple may process platform diagnostics and TestFlight feedback under your Apple settings.

This website does not use custom analytics, advertising trackers, signup forms, or embedded third-party fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes technical requests under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Purposes and legal bases

Where we process personal information, the legal bases are performance of a contract for providing and restoring purchased functionality; legitimate interests in answering enquiries, preventing fraud, and operating reliable services; and compliance with legal obligations where required. Optional permissions can be withdrawn through device settings. We do not sell your personal information.

Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub, and our email provider may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their applicable agreements and privacy policies describe the safeguards and transfer arrangements used.

Retention, deletion, and your rights

Local work records remain until deleted or replaced in the app or removed from the device. To remove the app’s local data, use iOS Delete App; offloading is different. Manage device backups and exported files separately. Uninstalling does not automatically delete purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat, which may be retained for purchase verification, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

You can export a JSON backup of saved data. We cannot access or recover work records stored only on your device. Restore purchases restores Pro access separately from data restoration.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information we hold, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us using the email above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority, including Datatilsynet in Denmark.

Children and policy changes

TapMyHours is a general work-time tool and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us. We will update this page when our practices change, including before introducing materially different data use.
