Free and Pro

Free includes unlimited work sessions and history, timer controls, multiple sessions per day, manual entries, notes, editing, daily and weekly totals, optional reminders, and JSON backup/restore.

Pro adds schedule-based time balance, an opening balance, estimated gross earnings from hourly rates, period reports, and PDF/CSV exports. Setup, calculations, and reports for these Pro features require an active Pro entitlement. You retain control of your saved work records.

Hours, balance, and earnings

You are responsible for reviewing the records and settings you enter. Time balance compares recorded work against your configured schedule from your chosen start date, plus any opening balance. Completed-day balance ends at yesterday; today’s progress is shown separately.

Holidays, sickness, leave, contractual overtime entitlements, and special pay premiums are not automatically applied. Earnings are estimates before tax based on recorded hours and configured hourly rates. The app does not calculate payroll, pension, deductions, net salary, or currency conversion. Amounts in different currencies should not be treated as one combined monetary total.

TapMyHours is a personal record-keeping tool, not legal, tax, accounting, or payroll advice. It does not certify compliance with employment rules or replace your employer’s required reporting process.

Your records and backups

Records are stored locally; there is no developer-operated cloud sync. Keep a separate JSON backup, especially before importing data, reinstalling, or changing devices. A PDF or CSV report is a readable export, not the app’s restoration format.

Device loss, app deletion, or unavailable backups may result in lost records. We cannot recover work data stored only on your device. Review import confirmation carefully, as restoring another backup can replace current data.
