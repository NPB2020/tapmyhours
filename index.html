A few useful answers

Before you get started.

Is this for salaried or hourly work?

Both. Use Free to record actual hours. With Pro, salaried employees can compare recorded time with their schedule; hourly workers can add an hourly rate for an estimate before tax. It is a personal record, not a payroll service.

Can I work more than once on the same day?

Yes. Stop after one session and start again later. TapMyHours adds the sessions together for the daily total.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. Core time tracking uses local data and works offline. Purchasing, validating, or restoring Pro can require an internet connection.

Where are my work records stored?

On your device. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Use JSON backup to keep a separate copy. Restore purchases restores Pro access, not your work history. Apple-managed device backups may also include app data, depending on your settings.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a non-consumable, one-time lifetime purchase for TapMyHours. You can continue recording unlimited work sessions without buying Pro.

Can it replace my employer's time system?

TapMyHours is designed for your personal overview. Follow your employer's reporting requirements; the app does not certify employment-law compliance or calculate contractual payroll entitlements.
