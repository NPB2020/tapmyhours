More than one session per day

Tap Start work when your day begins, Pause for a break, and Resume when you return. Stop saves the session. Working again later? Start a new session. Daily totals combine that day’s recorded work.

Add or correct a missed entry

Tap Add time, choose the work date from the calendar, and enter start/finish times or use a duration-only entry. Use duration-only when you know the total but not the exact times. Add a note if helpful.

Edit an existing entry from History. Check for duplicate work before saving a duration-only entry: without exact times, the app cannot fully check whether it overlaps another session.

Understand your time balance

With Pro, open Settings → Set up balance & earnings. Add contracted weekly hours, selected weekdays, a balance start date, and an optional positive or negative opening balance. Use effective dates when your schedule changes.

Balance equals your opening balance plus recorded work minus expected hours through yesterday. Today’s progress is separate. Holidays, sickness, and leave are not applied automatically.

Understand earnings estimates

Add an hourly rate and currency in Pro setup. Use effective-dated changes to retain earlier rates. Duration-only entries count as recorded work too. Estimates are before tax and exclude payroll deductions and automatic overtime premiums. The app does not convert currencies.

Restore a Pro purchase

Open TapMyHours Pro and select Restore purchases using the Apple account that made the original purchase. Check your internet connection. If access does not return, contact support with the app version and any visible error message.

Restoring Pro does not restore work records. Purchase testing is unavailable in the current preview; genuine purchase/restore testing requires the supported native iPhone version.

Back up and restore work records

Open Settings → Backup & restore. Export a JSON backup and keep it somewhere safe. Before importing an older file, back up your current records. Review the import confirmation carefully because restoring a backup can replace current data.

PDF and CSV reports are not JSON backups and cannot replace one for data restoration. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Apple-managed device backups are separate.

Export a PDF or CSV report

With Pro, open reports from Overview or Settings. Select a month, year, or custom period, then choose PDF or CSV and a destination in the iPhone share sheet. Review the selected dates and totals before sharing.

Duration-only entries are shown without invented start/finish times. If sharing does not open, include the format, date range, and any error message in your support email. Native sharing and PDF layout are being verified before release.

Check reminders

Enable Daily reminder in Settings and choose a time. Check iOS notification permission for TapMyHours and your Focus settings if a reminder does not appear. Turning reminders off does not delete your work history.

Privacy, deletion, and refunds

See the Privacy Policy for local storage, exported files, and deletion. Use iOS Delete App to remove local app data; manage backups and exported copies separately. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com. Read our Terms of Use for purchase and service terms.
